A few things to know

Before your
first week.

When does everyone read the updates?

The weekly release opens on Monday at midnight in your circle’s time zone. Before then, you can edit your own saved update while friends’ current updates remain hidden in the app.

Do I have to share to read?

A successfully saved update normally unlocks that week’s release once it opens. If you miss a week after joining, you can request access from a poster after release.

Who can join my circle?

The friends you invite. Lore has no public discovery feed or follower count. Members can deliberately export a readable preview, so shared copies can leave the circle.

What can I put in an update?

Words, photos, video and voice. Bring small moments together into one update, then comment and save moments after the release.

How do I try Lore?

Lore is currently an iPhone beta on Apple’s TestFlight. An iCloud account and internet access are required for shared features. Check TestFlight for current device compatibility.
