Put your week together

Add words, photos, video or voice. Save your update before the circle’s deadline; successful upload confirms participation.

Read after the release

Friends’ current updates stay hidden in the app until Monday in the circle’s time zone. Participation normally opens that week’s release; after a missed week, you can request access.

Keep the conversation going

Comment on a released update and save a moment to return to. Members can deliberately export readable content, so a private circle is not a promise that no one can make a copy.
