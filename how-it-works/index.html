How Lore works

Keep a little of your life through the week. Catch up with your circle when the release opens.

Invite your circle.

Start a circle and invite the friends who will share it.

Save your week.

Add words, photos, video or voice, then save your update before the weekly deadline.

Catch up. Then you’re caught up.

On Monday, read the week your friends shared. Leave a comment, save a moment, and reach the end of the release.

A shared weekly rhythm.

Your circle’s release opens Monday at midnight in its time zone. Until then, you can read and edit your own saved update. Friends’ current-week updates stay hidden in the app.

Share to read.

A successfully saved update normally gives you access to that week’s release once it opens. A draft or upload still in progress doesn’t count. If you miss a week after joining, you can ask a poster for access after release; earlier history has separate membership checks.

Keep the conversation going.

Read at your own pace, comment, react and save moments. Weeks, Months and Saved help you find your way back. Exporting a readable preview can send a copy outside your circle; opening a Lore link still follows the app’s access checks.
