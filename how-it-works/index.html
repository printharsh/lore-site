A different weekly rhythm

A little all week.
Together on Monday.

Real updates from your friends, with a beginning and an end.

01

Bring your people together.

Create a private circle or join an invitation from a friend. There’s no public feed to keep up with.

Choose the host together: everyone’s shared content uses the circle creator’s iCloud storage.

Start or join a circle
☀️The Sunday people
02

Keep a little of your week.

Words, photos, video, voice. Add one moment or tell the longer story, and come back as the week unfolds.

In the current beta, tap Save and wait for confirmation. A private draft or upload in progress doesn’t count as a shared update.

03

Open the week on Monday.

Your circle’s release opens Monday at midnight, in its time zone. Before then, friends’ current updates stay hidden in the app.

A successfully saved update normally gives you access. Missed the week? You can ask a poster for access after release; earlier history has separate membership checks.

04

Catch up. Keep the good bits.

Read at your own pace. Jump between friends, leave a reply, react, or save a moment to come back to.

Weeks, Months and Saved keep the history close. Exported previews can leave the circle; opening a Lore link still follows the app’s access checks.
