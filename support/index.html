Effective 2026-11-17 · Operated by Harsh Patel

Questions, a problem with your circle, or a safety concern? Email support@joinlore.io.

Find an answer

Browse the guides for joining a circle, saving an update, weekly releases, comments and notifications. Lore is currently available through TestFlight for iPhone.

Get help with the app

Tell us what happened, what you expected, your Lore version and roughly when the issue occurred. Screenshots can help, but remove anything private that is not needed. Never send passwords or Apple verification codes. You can also send beta feedback from Lore’s page in TestFlight.

Report a safety concern

Email the concern and the minimum context needed to understand it. Where an in-app Report action is available, review the report draft before sending it through Mail. Opening or saving a draft does not send it, and a source reference does not give support access to your private circle.

We review reports, may ask for relevant context, and respond using the remedies available for the issue. These can include help with blocking or leaving a circle, an app correction, or coordination with a circle owner. We do not automatically join private circles or have a general console that can read or erase their content.

Do not use Lore support for an emergency. If someone is in immediate danger, contact the emergency services where they are.

Control your experience

Use available blocking and circle controls if someone is making you uncomfortable. A personal block hides that person’s content for you; it does not erase their content or remove them for everyone. Leaving a circle and removing a member do not recall copies already shared or exported.

Privacy and deletion

For access, correction or deletion questions, email support@joinlore.io. Our privacy notice explains storage, sharing and the current deletion limits. Please identify the request without sending your Apple account credentials.
