Choose how to keep up

A different place for different moments.

A weekly catch-up, a photo journal, a glance at your Home Screen or a message right now. Start with the way your friends already want to share.

This is a guide to each approach, not an exhaustive feature comparison or a ranking. Product descriptions were reviewed on November 17, 2026. Check the official listings for current features and availability.

Lore

When you want everyone’s week to arrive together

Build an ordered update with words, photos, video and voice. Save it for an invited circle, then read the release from Monday at midnight in the circle’s time zone. Comments and saved moments keep their connection to the original update.

Try it for: friends in different cities who want the longer version of each other’s week without coordinating a call.

The commitment: in the current iPhone TestFlight beta, tap Save and wait for upload confirmation. Normal release access depends on contributing an update; a member who missed the week can request access after release. Friends’ current updates stay hidden in the app until release. It is a weekly catch-up, not immediate messaging.

Retro

When a photo journal is your starting point

Retro’s developer describes a weekly photo journal, adding existing photos and videos, monthly recaps, private group albums and group messaging. Its photo-journal approach is worth exploring if you want to collect what you photographed and share it with friends.

Consider it for: the group that naturally tells its week through a camera roll, or wants to explore shared albums alongside personal photo updates.

Retro’s official App Store description
Locket

When the small glance is the point

Locket’s developer centers the experience on friends’ photos appearing on your Home Screen. The listing also describes reactions, photo history and monthly recaps.

Consider it for: friends who enjoy a photo appearing as part of their everyday phone use, without making every image a longer account of the week.

Locket’s official App Store description
Your existing chat

When the conversation needs to happen now

You do not have to move every interaction into a new app. Keep plans, quick questions and the ongoing conversation in the chat your friends already use. A weekly update can sit alongside it when a story needs more room.

Consider staying with chat: if another app or a shared weekly deadline would feel like unnecessary work for the group. Lore does not import your chat history or replace an urgent-contact channel.

Using Lore alongside a group chat

Ask the group before choosing

  1. Do we want to share at any moment, or make time for a weekly catch-up?
  2. Do we mainly want photos, or do we want to tell a longer story with writing and voice too?
  3. Will everyone actually install and use the app? For Lore, that currently means iPhone, TestFlight and iCloud for shared features.
  4. Does contributing before reading feel motivating or inconvenient? Lore’s participation rule should be a choice everyone understands.
