Privacy in Lore

Effective 2026-11-17 · Operated by Harsh Patel

Lore helps invited friends share weekly updates. This notice explains information handled by the app, this website and support.

Your profile and private circles

Lore uses your iCloud account to recognize you. You choose the profile name and photo shown to your circles. Apple’s CloudKit stores profile information and shared circle data, including updates, photos, videos, voice recordings, members, comments, reactions, saved moments, and records needed for access and reading progress.

Circle content uses private and shared iCloud storage. The developer does not have a general dashboard for reading everyone’s circles. Being separately invited gives the developer the same kind of access as another member. The app applies membership and weekly-release rules; these are not a promise of separate server-enforced secrecy between people who already have access to the shared CloudKit storage.

Private writing and permissions

Your device keeps drafts, pending uploads, recovery information, downloaded media and preferences. A private draft or an upload in progress is not confirmation of a successful shared update. Camera, Photos and microphone permissions support the features you choose; Lore does not automatically upload your whole photo library.

For a selected photo or video, Lore can use date and location metadata to help fill in context. Coordinates from that selected item may be sent to Apple’s location service to suggest a place name. This is not continuous location tracking. A date or place you include in your update is shared with its readers.

On supported devices, voice transcription uses Apple’s on-device speech tools and may require a language-model download. Lore does not send recordings to a separate transcription or AI provider. Manage device permissions in iPhone Settings.

Sharing and notifications

People in a circle can keep or export content they can access. A shared preview can contain a name, image or video thumbnail, and text excerpt visible outside Lore, even when the original Lore link still requires membership and release access. Lore cannot recall screenshots, exports or copies kept in other apps.

Notifications can show names and activity on your device. Apple, connectivity and your settings affect delivery. Use your iPhone’s notification and preview settings to control alerts and lock-screen detail.

Support and safety reports

Support receives your email address, message and information you choose to send. In-app reports begin as drafts on your device that you review before sending through Mail. They do not automatically attach a whole update or its photos, video or voice. Removing a Lore draft does not remove a sent email or a separate Mail draft.

We use support information to investigate, respond and follow up. Our email providers, Porkbun and Google, process it for our hosted and forwarded support inboxes. We keep it while reasonably needed for those purposes or applicable legal obligations, then remove unnecessary copies. You can ask about access, correction or deletion at support@joinlore.io.

Apple may make feedback, screenshots and crash information you submit through TestFlight available to the developer. Include only what is needed to explain the issue.

This website and optional analytics

GitHub Pages hosts this website. Hosting services receive technical request information, such as your IP address, to deliver and secure pages. Interactive examples use fictional people and content. Anything you type into an example stays in that page and is lost when it reloads.

Optional Google Analytics helps us understand visits to public pages, which page variations are useful, and whether visitors choose Get Lore. It stays off unless you choose Allow analytics. With permission, we send limited page and interaction events, a page-variation identifier where applicable, and broad campaign or referral categories. Page addresses exclude query strings; we do not send the full referring address, private updates, names, email addresses, circle identifiers or text you enter into an example.

Google receives network information, including your IP address and browser or device information, when its service loads or receives events. Analytics cookies can recognize repeat visits. They last up to 30 days, and some renew during visits while you allow analytics. Your browser also keeps permitted campaign categories for the current session. These records are not a promise of anonymity. Read how Google uses information from sites that use its services and Google’s privacy policy.

You can refuse without losing website features and change your choice using Analytics settings in the footer. Turning analytics off stops future collection from this website and clears its analytics cookies and stored campaign categories. A request containing events collected while you had given permission may already be in transit and finish afterward. Turning analytics off does not erase events already received by Google. Your browser remembers your consent choice until you change it or clear site data. We do not enable Google Signals, advertising personalization or sharing with Google advertising products.

Contact support@joinlore.io with an analytics access or deletion request. We do not connect analytics records to your name or email. While analytics is still on, choose Show analytics reference in Analytics settings and copy that reference into your request before turning analytics off. This reference identifies the browser’s analytics records; a different browser or previously cleared cookie may have a different reference. Without the relevant reference, some requests may not be identifiable. Do not send private circle content. We will explain what we can locate and remove; clearing browser data alone does not remove records already collected. We set Google Analytics’ detailed user and event retention to two months without resetting it on new activity. Google processes expired records through its deletion cycle; aggregated reports may remain longer.

App analytics and purchases

App analytics collection is disabled in the currently available app. Lore does not use an advertising SDK or track you across other companies’ apps and websites for advertising.

Where optional tips are available, Apple handles the purchase. Lore receives purchase confirmation, not payment-card details. Tips do not unlock access to your circles. Apple’s privacy and purchase terms also apply to its services.

Keeping and deleting information

In-app account deletion is not enabled in the currently available version. Contact support@joinlore.io about a deletion request. Support cannot directly erase arbitrary private iCloud circles. A circle owner’s storage can contain other people’s updates, which cannot be treated as the owner’s personal content.

Shared information can remain in iCloud until the applicable removal process finishes. Leaving a circle, removing a member or uninstalling Lore does not by itself erase content already shared or exported. Your device may retain drafts and downloaded copies. Use available app and device controls, and contact us if you need help understanding a specific request.

Questions and changes

Contact support@joinlore.io for privacy questions. If our handling of information changes, we will update this notice and its effective date. Where a new choice or permission is required, we will ask for it.
