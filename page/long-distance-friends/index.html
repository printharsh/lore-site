One deadline, wherever you are

Your circle keeps one time zone and one Monday release. Check that deadline together when you create the circle; it may fall on a different local date or hour for some friends.

Show what changed around you

A photo of the new apartment, the route you walk to work, a voice note after a difficult day. Put them in the same update so friends get the context as well as the headline.

Leave room for a reply

Read when you have time and comment on the exact moment you want to ask about. Everyone does not need to be online together.

How to use Lore for this

  1. Create a circle and check its time zone together.
  2. Add the small scenes from your week and explicitly save before the deadline.
  3. Read the Monday release when you have time, then reply to a particular moment.

Is it a fit?

A good fit when schedules rarely line up but everyone wants a recurring catch-up.

Keep calls and urgent messages elsewhere; Lore opens shared updates on the weekly release.

Lore is currently an iPhone beta through TestFlight. Shared features need iCloud and internet access.

Questions before you start

Do we all have to be online on Monday?

No. Monday is when the release opens. Eligible members can read and comment later.

What if we live in different time zones?

The circle uses one time zone, selected at creation. Its Monday midnight may be a different local time for each friend.

Can I see an update as soon as my friend saves it?

Their current update stays hidden in the app until release. Saving is not an early preview.
