How to use Lore for this

  1. Create an invited circle with adult relatives.
  2. Help everyone try saving an update before the first deadline.
  3. Open the release, reply and save the family moments you want to revisit.

Is it a fit?

Best for adult relatives comfortable sharing something each week on iPhone.

For adult relatives who each want to take part, rather than a one-way family broadcast. Keep backups of original media.

Lore is currently an iPhone beta through TestFlight. Shared features need iCloud and internet access.

Questions before you start

Can relatives without iPhones join?

Lore is currently an iPhone beta through TestFlight. There is no app-free circle reader promised here.

Can someone only read?

Normal weekly access depends on a saved update. A member who misses a week can request access after release.

Are our photos impossible to share outside the family?

No. Invitations limit the intended audience, but members can export or copy content they can read.
