How to use Lore for this

  1. Agree that a weekly shared update suits both of you.
  2. Invite each other and check the circle’s release time.
  3. Save before the deadline, then read and respond after release.

Is it a fit?

Fits a couple who both want a weekly record alongside calls and messages.

A poor fit if either person needs every update delivered immediately or does not want a participation deadline.

Lore is currently an iPhone beta through TestFlight. Shared features need iCloud and internet access.

Questions before you start

Can we still use our usual messenger?

Yes. Lore can sit alongside calls and everyday messages.

Can I read their update during the week?

Friends’ current updates stay hidden in the app until the release opens; your own remains editable during the open week.

Does Lore guarantee a notification for every change?

No. Notification permission, device settings, network conditions and Apple delivery affect alerts.
