How to use Lore for this

  1. Create or join an invited circle.
  2. Add photos and their context, then save the update.
  3. After Monday’s release, reply to and save particular moments.

Is it a fit?

Useful when photos matter most with a little explanation for close friends.

Not a public portfolio, a guaranteed backup service or a promise that recipients cannot copy media.

Lore is currently an iPhone beta through TestFlight. Shared features need iCloud and internet access.

Questions before you start

Can I share only photos?

Yes. You can also add text, video and voice when those explain the moment better.

Is there a public feed?

Lore is built around invited circles, not a public discovery feed.

Do saved moments replace my original photo library?

No. They help revisit content in Lore and remain subject to source access. Keep your originals.
