Is it a fit?

Useful when quick messages are frequent but personal updates disappear in the scroll.

Lore does not import your message history or replace urgent group messaging.

Lore is currently an iPhone beta through TestFlight. Shared features need iCloud and internet access.

Questions before you start

Does Lore replace our group chat?

It does not need to. Use chat for plans and quick replies, and Lore for the weekly update with context.

Can everyone talk under an update?

Members who can read a released update can comment and react. The conversation is not limited to an author-only exchange.

What happens when someone misses a week?

After release, a member who missed the week can request access from a poster. Approval opens that release without adding participation credit.
