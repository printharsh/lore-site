How to use Lore for this

  1. Choose text, voice or a mix in your weekly update.
  2. Add context and save before the circle’s deadline.
  3. Read and listen after release, with comments attached to the relevant moment.

Is it a fit?

Fits stories that do not translate into a single photo.

Voice takes listening time. Consider your friends’ setting and add written context when useful.

Questions before you start

Do I need to use every media type?

No. Choose the format that fits your update.

Can someone reply to a specific part?

Released updates support conversations about particular moments, including text, photo or voice replies where available in the beta.

Are voice updates available immediately?

Shared current-week content follows the same weekly release and access rules as other media.
