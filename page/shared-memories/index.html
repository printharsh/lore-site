Questions before you start

Are Weeks, Months and Saved separate copies?

They are different ways to find circle content. A saved item keeps its connection to the original update.

Can Saved reveal a week I cannot access?

No. Saving a reference does not bypass source-access rules.

Can I search only photos or voice?

The shared library search controls include media filters such as Photos and Voice, alongside Mine and Links.
