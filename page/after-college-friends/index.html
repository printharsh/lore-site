Lore is currently an iPhone beta through TestFlight. Shared features need iCloud and internet access.

Questions before you start

Is this only for graduates?

No. The same approach works after moving city or leaving a shared workplace.

Can I invite someone later?

You can invite friends to the circle. Reading history follows membership and release access checks; an invite is not a promise of every past update.

Can a short update count?

A successfully saved eligible update counts; it does not have to be a long essay.
