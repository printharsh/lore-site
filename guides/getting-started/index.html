Start or join a circle

Install the Lore iPhone beta, set up your profile, and join the friends you want to share with.

Before you startGet into your circleChoose who creates the circleInvite deliberately

Before you start

Join the Lore beta through Apple’s TestFlight on an iPhone with iOS 18 or later. You need an iCloud account and an internet connection for shared features. Places are limited; the TestFlight page shows current availability. Expect rough edges, keep copies of irreplaceable media, and send problems through TestFlight feedback.

Get into your circle

  1. Install TestFlight and the Lore beta, then open Lore.
  2. Choose your profile photo and name. They help your circle recognise your updates and comments.
  3. Create a circle for your friends, or open the invitation someone sent you and follow Apple’s sharing prompts.
  4. If you installed Lore after receiving an invitation, open the original invitation again to continue joining.

Choose who creates the circle

The circle creator’s iCloud stores the circle’s shared content, including updates from friends. It uses the creator’s available iCloud storage. Agree who will host it before you begin.

If the creator’s iCloud is full, uploads may fail or stay pending. Wait for a successful Save confirmation before treating an update as shared. Keep the original photos and videos you care about; a beta circle should not be their only copy.

Invite deliberately

A circle is invitation-only. There is no public feed or directory to join from. Use the circle’s sharing controls to manage invitations; being sent a screenshot or exported preview is not circle membership.

You can belong to more than one circle. Check which circle you are in before writing—Lore does not automatically publish your update across circles.

These guides describe the current TestFlight beta. The interactive website preview is illustrative.
