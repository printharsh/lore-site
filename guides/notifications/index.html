Check your notifications

Check iPhone permissions and Focus, understand activity banners, and report a missing Lore notification.

Check the basicsWhat a banner can tell youIf something is missing

Check the basics

  1. Open Lore after installing or updating it so the app can refresh its account and notification setup.
  2. In iPhone Settings → Notifications → Lore, check that notifications are allowed and choose the alerts you want.
  3. Check whether Focus or Scheduled Summary is delaying the banner. Also make sure the phone can connect to the internet.
  4. Open the relevant circle to see whether the activity itself arrived in the app.

What a banner can tell you

Post and edit activity can arrive as a direct notification. When Lore can verify the sender and circle, the notification can name them; “New activity in Lore” remains a fallback. A mention or reply still has to follow the source’s release and access rules.

Notifications can arrive while the app is not onscreen, but permission, network conditions and Apple’s delivery behaviour affect whether and when they appear. A recorded nudge is not proof that a banner reached the phone.

If something is missing

Send feedback through TestFlight with the approximate time, the kind of activity and your Lore version. Say whether the activity appeared when you opened the app, and whether Focus was on. Do not include passwords, verification codes or someone else’s private media.

TestFlight’s new-build notifications are separate from activity inside Lore. Updating the app and receiving a friend’s activity banner are different events.
