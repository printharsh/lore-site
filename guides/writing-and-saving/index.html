One update, any mix of moments

Your weekly update can be text-only, photo-led, a voice note, or a longer mix of text, photos, video and voice. You can add to it during the open week.

Save in the current beta

  1. Open your current-week update in the circle you want to share with.
  2. Write or add media. Type @ to mention someone in that circle, or add a caption and more context to a photo.
  3. Tap Save and wait for Lore to confirm it has saved. A private draft, an upload in progress or a failed attempt does not count as participation.
  4. Come back before the deadline to edit or add more, and save those changes too.

If saving is interrupted

Keep your draft and follow the retry or recovery state Lore shows. Closing the composer keeps private work. Do not delete a draft to try to force a failed upload through.

The beta’s Save action is explicit. Automatic weekly sharing is not enabled in the currently delivered version. Your update remains hidden from friends until the release opens, even after saving succeeds.

Adding from another app

Use the iPhone share sheet to send supported photos, videos, links or text to Lore. Choose the intended circle and review the imported items. Sharing something into Lore does not publish it automatically; review and Save are still required.
