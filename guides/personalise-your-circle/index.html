Make the circle feel like yours

Choose a cover, background, circle icon and font, and preview how they work together.

Choose the right surface

Open Circle settings. The cover belongs to the header; the background sits behind the circle’s content. Their pickers share the same catalogue, but the previews show those different uses.

Build your circle’s look

  1. Choose a cover and use its preview to check the header controls against it.
  2. Choose a background and check the sample post for readability. You can use catalogue favourites and available scale or intensity controls.
  3. Choose an emoji, colour and shape for the circle icon.
  4. Preview a font style, then save. Circle typography applies to Lore’s own text; Apple’s system sheets keep their system type.

Keep your changes

Use Save in a customization sheet. Cover, background and name changes stay staged until you also Save Circle settings. If a conflict appears, review it rather than assuming the new version has already been saved.

Your iPhone accessibility settings still matter. Lore supports dark mode, larger text, reduced transparency, increased contrast and reduced motion. Choose artwork that remains readable with the settings your friends use.
