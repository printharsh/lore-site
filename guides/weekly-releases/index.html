Understand releases and access

Find the weekly deadline, understand why a release is locked, and request access after a missed week.

The deadline belongs to the circleRead the finished releaseIf you missed a weekAfter the week closes

The deadline belongs to the circle

The week stays open through Sunday at 11:59 PM in the circle’s time zone. Monday at midnight closes that release and starts the next composition week. Friends in different time zones still share that same circle deadline.

Before release, you can see and edit your own saved update. Your friends’ current-week content remains hidden in the app. Saving your update does not reveal it early.

Read the finished release

  1. Confirm your update saved successfully for that week. Participation normally gives you access once the release opens.
  2. Open the finished release from the home screen, or go to Lore → Weeks to find a past release.
  3. Read at your own pace. The reader’s people picker and Next control help you move between longer updates.

If you missed a week

After release, use Request access when Lore offers it. One eligible poster’s approval opens that particular release. It does not create a submission, posting credit or access to a different week.

Current members may also have access to history from before they joined, when Lore can verify their joining time. If the joining time or your participation cannot be verified, Lore should not assume access.

After the week closes

Released update content is fixed, while its conversation remains available. Keep new writing in the new week. A retry of an earlier upload belongs to its original week; it does not also submit an update for the new week.
