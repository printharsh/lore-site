Reply to and save a moment

Comment on a released update, react to a specific moment, and find saved context again.

Start with the momentReact without losing your placeFind what you savedSharing outside the circle

Start with the moment

  1. Open a released update you can read.
  2. Use the speech-bubble action to open Comments. You can also respond to a specific text, photo, video or voice moment and keep that source context attached.
  3. Leave text, a photo or a voice reply. Current eligible circle members who can read the source can join the conversation.

React without losing your place

Choose emoji or a circle sticker in the reaction picker. Tap a selected reaction again to remove it, then use Done when you are finished. If a save fails, Lore can restore the acknowledged reaction state and show retry feedback.

Find what you saved

Open Lore → Saved to find saved moments. Search there matches the saved moment, rather than unrelated parts of its original update. Weeks and Months help you find whole releases.

A saved moment keeps a connection to its source. Reading the complete original update still depends on your current access; saving one moment does not unlock everything around it.

Sharing outside the circle

An exported photo/text preview can be viewed or forwarded outside Lore. The original Lore link still follows circle and release checks. Only export content you intend to let leave the circle.
